I am a staff software engineer who builds distributed data, simulation, and deployment platforms for defense research programs. At the [University of Dayton Research Institute](https://udayton.edu/udri/), I take systems from stakeholder requirements through architecture, deployment, and operational support, while guiding technical decisions for multidisciplinary teams of 10–15 people.

My background in [Digital Simulation and Game Engineering Technology](https://www.shawnee.edu/areas-study/college-professional-studies/engineering-technologies/bachelor-degrees/game-programming) from [Shawnee State University](https://www.shawnee.edu/) continues to inform my work in XR training, real-time simulation, and human-centered software. I also build independent AI orchestration and self-hosted model infrastructure using Python, Docker, and OpenAI-compatible APIs.
