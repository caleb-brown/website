# Description

Our Cafe is a mobile-first, real-time cafe operations app for managing orders across a shared counter. Customers can browse the drink menu and place customized orders, while staff can claim orders and advance them through brewing, ready, and completed states from any connected device.

## Highlights

- Live order and inventory updates across connected clients
- Customizable drink recipes, modifiers, notes, and ingredient tracking
- Staff workflow for claiming and advancing orders through preparation
- Local DuckDB persistence with a containerized deployment option

## Technologies

- React, TypeScript, and Vite
- Express and Socket.IO
- DuckDB
- Tailwind CSS and Framer Motion
- Docker Compose
